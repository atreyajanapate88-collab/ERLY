Privacy

This is a plain-language overview of the information ERLY uses to provide its features. The operator of the ERLY instance is responsible for its configuration and data.

Information ERLY uses

  • Discord account details returned during sign-in, such as your user ID, username, display name, avatar, and email when Discord provides it.
  • Discord server names, IDs, icons, permissions, and approximate counts needed to show and verify servers you select.
  • ER:LC server details and settings you submit, including the private server API key, display name, and welcome message.
  • Server activity data needed to deliver configured welcome messages and show delivery status.

How information is used and shared

  • Account and server information is used to sign you in, verify access, and provide ERLY management features.
  • ERLY sends requests to Discord and ER:LC services when needed to authenticate, verify servers, or run the features you configure.
  • ERLY does not sell personal information.

Storage and retention

  • Sign-in sessions are held in server memory for up to one year. Signing out ends the current session.
  • Saved server settings are encrypted in the ERLY instance's local configuration store and remain until the instance operator removes them.
  • Storage practices may differ for deployments operated by others. Contact the operator of your ERLY instance for data access or deletion requests.

Your choices and third-party services

You can sign out of ERLY, remove its access through your Discord account settings, or ask your server administrator to remove the ERLY bot. Discord and ER:LC services have their own privacy policies and terms.

This overview is a starting point for the ERLY preview and is not legal advice. The instance operator should review it and provide any additional contact or jurisdiction details before public deployment.
